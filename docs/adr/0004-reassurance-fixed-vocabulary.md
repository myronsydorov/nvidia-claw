# ADR-0004: Private reassurance uses a fixed vocabulary over an end-to-end encrypted relay

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
The layer-2 promise is "peace of mind without surveillance." Free-text answers generated by an LLM could leak details ("she's at the hospital on 5th street").

## Decision
- Answers are restricted to `level` × `reason` enums (CONTRACTS §2). The sender's Warden rejects anything else **before** encryption.
- Transport: X25519 sealed boxes through a dumb relay that stores only ciphertext.
- The watched person owns the sharing rules and sees a log of every question asked about them.

## Consequences
- ✅ The privacy claim can be proven: the privacy receipt plus the OpenShell egress log show a single small encrypted message.
- ⚠️ Less expressive answers, on purpose. Richer updates happen human-to-human ("call her").
